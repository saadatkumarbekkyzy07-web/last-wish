LAST WISH — LAPTOP EDITION
==========================
HOW TO PLAY
  Double-click "Last Wish.html" (opens in Edge / Chrome). No internet needed.
  Keep the "models", "images" and "videos" folders next to it.

MAIN MENU
  ▶ Play       - the story: a map of 4 nights and 2 Moon quizzes
  👥 2 Players  - Monkey vs Mega cockroach on one keyboard (split screen)
  🛍 Market     - buy and wear costumes with the ★ stars you earned

THE MAP (1 player)
  Night 1 → Night 2 → Moon Quiz 1 → Night 3 → Night 4 → Moon Quiz 2 → The End
  ★ Stars: finish a night in under 3:00 = 3 ★, under 5:00 = 2 ★, slower = 1 ★
           (replaying a night only adds stars if you beat your best)
  ♥ Moon lives: pass a Moon quiz (4 of 5 right) = ♥ 1, perfect score = ♥ 2.
           If the monkey falls during a night, a life gets it back up.
  No lives left? The Moon gives one second-chance quiz. Fail it and End 2 plays;
  the map starts again, but your ★ stars and costumes are kept.
  Progress is saved automatically (in this browser).

CONTROLS (1 player)
  W A S D move · Mouse look · Shift run · Space jump
  Left click / J hit · Right click / K kick · Q punch · F leg punch · R ULTRA · E door · Esc pause

CONTROLS (2 players)
  🐒 Monkey:          W/S walk · A/D turn · Left Shift run · Space jump
                      F hit · G kick · Q punch · E leg punch · R ULTRA (3)
  🪳 Mega cockroach:  ↑/↓ walk · ←/→ turn · Enter bite · Right Shift charge

CHANGING THINGS
  All numbers (timers, stars, prices, damage...) are in the SETTINGS part near the top of the
  code inside "Last Wish.html" (open it with Notepad, search for "GAME SETTINGS").
