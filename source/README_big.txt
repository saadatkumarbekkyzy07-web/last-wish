LAST WISH — BIG SCREEN EDITION  (touch wall, e.g. 7680 x 2160)
================================
HOW TO PLAY
  Copy this WHOLE folder to the screen's computer.
  Double-click "Last Wish - Big Screen.html" (opens in Edge). No internet, keyboard or mouse needed.
  Tap "Play" or "2 Players" - the game switches to full screen by itself.

ON-SCREEN BUTTONS - just TAP them, no holding:
  ▲ start walking (tap again to stop)  ▼ walk back  ◀ ▶ step sideways  ⟲ ⟳ turn 45° per tap
  ■ STOP  RUN on/off   ·  Hit  Kick  Punch  Leg punch  ULTRA  Jump  Door  ·  ❚❚ pause

1 PLAYER   all buttons are together on the RIGHT side of the screen
2 PLAYERS  the screen is split: LEFT half + LEFT buttons = 🐒 Monkey,
           RIGHT half + RIGHT buttons = 🪳 Mega cockroach (Bite, Charge)

The map, stars, Moon lives and market work the same as the laptop edition.
Settings are inside the .html file (open with Notepad, search for "GAME SETTINGS").
